About this desk Dad, runner, former (but still occasionally active) national tour manager, trader.

Section

The name

KidRunComVC is four pieces stuck together that keep my priorities front and center: Kid, Run, Com, VC.

  1. 01 Kid, because I'm a dad and granddad first. The kids come first, whether they know it or not. Most (not all) of the risks I've taken, and still take, I take for them. Big, delusional goals take a long time, and you have to be willing to fail in public, or at least look like you're failing, while you study, incubate, and build. If failing were the goal, I'd have retired early. If I had to do it all over again, I'd rather go crab fishing in the Bering Sea. It's safer.
  2. 02 Run, because the miles happen whether or not the kids, the injuries, or the charts cooperate.
  3. 03 Com, for the years as a national tour manager in the comedy business.

VC

venture capitalist

The goal

is the last piece of the handle. It stands for venture capitalist. It's not my job title yet, and it's not a pitch. It's my final delusional life goal. I set delusional goals on purpose, because I think this life is worth going for moonshots. It also marks the finish line of a long run as an entrepreneur.

Age 8

It started around age 8 with a cardboard vending machine I made arts-and-crafts style, complete with cut-out coin slots and a vending door. I sat inside it and sold to family and anyone who visited our house. In the early 1980s I sold USA Today subscriptions and candy door-to-door. I even went door-to-door asking people to donate things from their junk drawers, then sold those door-to-door too!

At 18

At 18, I started a landscaping company, and a year later a food truck. Both failed fast. After some years of working for a paycheck, I went back to trying, and failing, at various businesses.

Along the way

Along the way I was general manager of a country club and a Funny Bone comedy club, and regional manager over several group homes for at-risk youth. After that came tour managing, a restaurant, a tax-prep firm, and finally, as of today, full-time trading and blogging. These days I'm a student of the global markets, with a focus on technical analysis and derivatives.

The last stop

This is the last stop for me. Wish me luck!

I think this life is worth going for moonshots.
