About this desk

Dad, runner, comedian on the side, trader.

The name

KidRunComVC is four pieces stuck together: Kid, Run, Com, VC.

  • Kid, because this is a dad.
  • Run, because the miles happen whether or not the chart cooperates.
  • Com, because there is a comedian on the side.
  • VC is the last piece of the handle. It is part of the name, not a job title and not a pitch.

What shows up here

Market history and technical analysis, written so they can be read out loud without a glossary. A joke gets in when it has earned the space. Nothing on this site is a recommendation, a signal, or a reason to do anything with money.

If you came for a stock tip, the back button is the whole product. If you came for 1637 with the folklore scraped down, you are in the right room.

No real name, no city, no headshot. The essays are the introduction.
