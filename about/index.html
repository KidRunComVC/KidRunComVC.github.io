The name

KidRunComVC is four pieces stuck together that keep my priorities front and center: Kid, Run, Com, VC.

  1. 01 Kid, because I'm a dad first. The kids come first, whether they know it or not. Most (not all) of the risks I took, I took for them. Big, delusional goals take a long time, and you have to be willing to look like you're failing while you study, incubate, and build.
  2. 02 Run, because the miles happen whether or not the kids or the charts cooperate.
  3. 03 Com, for the years as a national tour manager in the comedy business.
  4. 04 VC is the last piece of the handle. It's part of the name, not a job title and not a pitch. It marks the finish line of a long run in the financial world, from tax-prep firm owner to full-time market participant. My current job, and my last one, is student of the global markets, with a focus on technical analysis and derivatives.

If I had to do it all over again, I'd rather go crab fishing in the Bering Sea. It's safer.
