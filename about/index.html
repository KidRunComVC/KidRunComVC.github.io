About this desk

Dad, runner, former (but still occasionally active) national tour manager, trader.

The name

KidRunComVC is four pieces stuck together that keep my priorities front and center: Kid, Run, Com, VC.

  • Kid, because I'm a dad first. The kids come first, whether they know it or not. Most of the risks I took, I took for them. If I had to do it all over again, I'd rather go crab fishing in the Bering Sea. It's safer.
  • Run, because the miles happen whether or not the kids or the charts cooperate.
  • Com, for the years as a national tour manager in the comedy business.
  • VC is the last piece of the handle. It's part of the name, not a job title and not a pitch. It marks the finish line of a long run in the financial world, from tax-prep firm owner to full-time market participant. My current job, and my last one, is student of the global markets, with a focus on technical analysis and derivatives.
