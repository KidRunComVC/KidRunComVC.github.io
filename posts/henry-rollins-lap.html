The Day Henry Rollins Sat on My Wife's Lap

KidRunComVC

In the summer of 2013, Lindsay and I were in Washington, DC, when Henry Rollins rolled into town to film his History Channel H2 show, 10 Things You Don't Know About. He was kind enough to let us be part of it. We interviewed him on the street about marriage equality, one of the biggest issues in the country at the time. More on that interview in a later post. Sometimes life drops you in exactly the right place at exactly the right time, and this was one of those moments.

Lindsay's segment was pure Lindsay, goofy and fearless on the outside. On the inside, she found Henry a little intimidating, and her crush was very real. Then, right in the middle of her scene, Henry Rollins sat down on her lap. Did I get a picture of it? Nope. If you want to see it, you'll have to watch the episode like everybody else. Consider it the least punk rock cliffhanger ever. Years later, we still have the photos, and the memories still make us smile.

The episode was "Prohibition," and it aired on November 23, 2013. In every photo from that day, my wife is grinning ear to ear.

I love Henry Rollins. My wife loves him more. That photo of the two of them is hers, lol.

You can watch the full episode, Season 2, Episode 4, on Prime Video.
