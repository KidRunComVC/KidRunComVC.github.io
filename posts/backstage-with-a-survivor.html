Backstage with a Survivor

A minute backstage with Rupert Boneham, and the photo to prove it.

KidRunComVC

I was the national tour manager on the Bob & Tom All-Stars Comedy Tour. After one show I ended up backstage with Rupert Boneham, which is not a sentence I had on the call sheet. He was the Survivor fan favorite who won the million-dollar viewer vote on Survivor: All-Stars in 2004. The beard entered the conversation first. I just tried to stand still and look like I belonged in the picture.
