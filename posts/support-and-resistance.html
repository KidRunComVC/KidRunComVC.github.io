Support and resistance

The chart's memory of a place where buyers or sellers dug in. A zone, not a spell.

KidRunComVC / 8 October 2026 / 3 minute read

Price is a negotiation that leaves fingerprints. Trade near the same neighborhood often enough and a chart starts to look as if it has opinions. Support is the name for a zone where declines have stalled. Resistance is the mirror image: a zone where rallies have stalled. That is the whole idea. The rest is manners, and a little caution.

A zone, not a razor

Draw these as bands, not as a price to the penny. A crowd does not change its mind at a single tick. It changes its mind around a prior high, a prior low, a round number someone can remember without a pencil. If you have to zoom until a perfect line appears, you have invented a pen pal.

Why the same neighborhoods return

Memory. Someone bought there and spent weeks wishing they had not, and a return to that price feels like a door. Someone sold there and felt clever, and a revisit looks like a second chance to feel clever again. Neither feeling is a law. Both show up often enough that the marks are worth seeing.

A level can also switch jobs. Resistance that finally gives way is sometimes treated, on a later visit, as support. The old ceiling gets tested as a floor. Sometimes. That word is doing all the honest work in the sentence. A chart does not owe anyone a role reversal because a textbook sketched one.

How to look without fooling yourself

Real charts are ruder than the sketch above. They poke through a zone, wander back, and dare you to explain it with a ruler. A mark that deserves the name has dull evidence: more than one pause, trading that actually happened there, and a zone you can describe out loud. One touch is an anecdote.

Nothing on this page is an instruction. A support zone is not a promise that price will bounce, and it is not a reason to do anything with money. It is a note about where a fight happened. The next fight is a new negotiation. It has heard of the last one. It is not bound by it.

The lesson

Support is a place the chart remembers a fight. It is not a contract that the same side will win again.
