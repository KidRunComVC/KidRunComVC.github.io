Battle Calls

How bouts are scored

A bout is one public caller against THE MARKET. The entry, the target, and the deadline come from the caller’s own post. Those three lines are the fight.

The call size

The call size is the percent move from the entry to the target. If a caller gives a range, only the lower number counts. A call from $100 to $120–$140 is a 20% call.

Caller’s KO

Caller’s KO is one penny past the target, in the caller’s direction. You have to go past the call by one penny to knock it out. A long from $100 to $120 has a Caller’s KO at $120.01. A short from $100 to $80 has a Caller’s KO at $79.99. A touch at Caller’s KO, or beyond it, is a knockout win for the caller.

THE MARKET’s KO

THE MARKET’s KO is that same distance, taken the other way from the entry, and then one penny further. A long from $100 to $120 has THE MARKET’s KO at $79.99. A short from $100 to $80 has THE MARKET’s KO at $120.01. A touch at THE MARKET’s KO, or beyond it, is a knockout win for THE MARKET.

The distance against the call stops at 50% of the entry. The extra penny still applies past that cap. A call for a 100% move is knocked out 50% the other way, plus one penny, not 100% the other way.

There is no stop on the card. THE MARKET’s KO is what ends a bout the hard way. A stop a caller named is not used for now.

A touch

A level is hit when price trades there. An intraday touch counts, for Caller’s KO and for THE MARKET’s KO. It does not have to be the close.

How it ends

  • Win by KO. Caller’s KO is touched before the deadline.
  • Win by decision. Caller’s KO is not touched, and the deadline close is past the entry in the caller’s direction.
  • Loss by KO. THE MARKET’s KO is touched before the deadline.
  • Loss by decision. The deadline passes with price on the wrong side of the entry.

The Price Decided and THE MARKET won.

Same day

If Caller’s KO and THE MARKET’s KO are both touched on the same day, the one that was hit first wins. That order comes from intraday minute data. If the order cannot be told, the bout is marked Under review until it can.

The clock

Entry is the price at the time of the post. When the post lands outside market hours, entry is the last regular-session trade. The deadline is the regular-session close on the deadline date.
