Gold: Cathie’s ‘Going Down’ vs. Stock Market TV Family’s Bet on Miners

Two smart camps reading the gold market differently.

KidRunComVC Nov 16, 2026 / 6-minute read

Round 1 is gold. On Nov 6, Cathie Wood said, “We think gold's going down.” On Nov 11, on Stock Market TV's Morning Show, Jason Perz, All Star Charts' Chief Commodities Strategist, said that if gold “really holds above 4,000, really 3,900,” it can be “very bullish,” and on the miners, “I think you want to be a buyer.” Co-host JC Parets said he “started buying gold stocks yesterday” (Nov 10). Neither side gave a target or a time frame, and they're not technically betting on the same thing (Cathie on the gold price, the Stock Market TV side on the mining stocks), but the rock is the asset that controls the ultimate outcome. Round 1 runs through Dec 31. Here's the tape, the rules, and the weekly score.

Rules of the Ring

Neither side gave a price target or a time frame. Nobody can be marked right or wrong on their own terms yet. The scoring above is my house rules, written down before later results pile up, so the card cannot drift after the fact.

  • Each call is scored weekly from that side's start date, using regular-session closes.
  • ARK Invest starts at the Nov 6, 2026 close: GLD 372.52 and GDX 87.60.
  • Stock Market TV starts at the Nov 10, 2026 close: GLD 369.36 and GDX 86.49. The Morning Show aired before the Nov 11 close, so the prior session is the fairer starting print.
  • Round 1 ends Dec 31, 2026.
  • Both sides are scored with GLD and with GDX, even though Cathie was talking about the gold price and Perz was talking about miners.
  • The table tracks direction only. It is not a verdict, and it is not investment advice.

Joey's Read

Full disclosure: I’m a fan of both corners. In my opinion, ARK Invest and Stock Market TV have some of the highest-quality people I’ve come across. They’re very different firms, so if you came here for a grudge match, I’m sorry to disappoint you. This is a friendly sparring session they didn’t sign up for - it’s just for fun.

Technically speaking, it’s not even a fair fight, which is part of the fun. Neither side put a hard target or a deadline on it, and one corner thinks the gold price keeps sliding while the other is bullish and putting money into gold miners. That’s not apples to apples. It’s more like apples to an apple orchard. Miners usually move with gold, just louder. At some big turns, like 2000 and 2008, they started up first, but other times, like early 2016, they bottomed after gold. So I watch them as a clue, not a guarantee. Since nobody set a clock, I’m setting one through Dec 31. Neither of them agreed to that deadline. Consider the scorecard what it is: a look at two smart camps reading the same shiny rock differently.

And in the spirit of honesty, this is my first battle post, so it’s really to help me figure out the format and hopefully improve on the next one. Feel free to message me your ideas or insults. Smarter folks will probably run better battles down the road. Plenty of people already keep score on pundits (shout-out to CXO’s Guru Grades and AlphaAudit). My spin is the fight-card format. I put the idea on the record Nov 16, 2026. Borrow it all you want, just give the old guy a little credit. 😄
